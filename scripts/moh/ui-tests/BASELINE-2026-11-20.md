## Headline findings
1. **Rotate overlay blocks dashboards 10 and 14 (all sampled tabs) and the dashboard list at <= 768 px.** Cause: responsive mode is an opt-in allowlist (dashboards 3 and 8) and `isDashboardPage()` also matches `/dashboard/list/`.
2. **Chart clipping on the pilot dashboards too**: dashboard 8 tab 4 clips 11 charts at 1440 px, 8 at 375; dashboard 3 tabs 1/4/5 clip at 1440. The pilot is not clean.
3. Tap targets under 24 px on every page type; nested-interactive and contrast are the largest axe groups.
4. **Render time**: a production dashboard needs about 25-40 s to finish rendering at 1440 px (measured; includes lazy charts). Each dashboard tab keeps about 3 loading placeholders until scrolled.

## Not covered (NOT VERIFIED)
- Tabs other than 1/4/5, widths 320/1024/1920/3840, training instance: the full profile exists (`MOH_UI_PROFILE=full`) but takes hours; not run.
- Pages this read-only account cannot see: SQL Lab, alerts, settings, admin.
- Manual checks: screen reader, focus order by a person, reduced motion, 200 % zoom, RTL/Amharic.
- Counts on a few late-loading tabs (dashboard 14 tab 5) vary between runs; budget is the max of two runs.

## Method notes (what changed while building this)
- First baselines were unreliable: pages were measured half-rendered. The settle wait now needs: mounted app, no spinner, DOM quiet 3 s, lazy-chart scroll sweep. Loaders that survive the sweep are recorded as PERF-STUCK-LOADER.
- Ratchet: exact for layout rules; 10 % or +2 for noisy rules; severe rules never allowed on unbudgeted pages.
