👋 Welcome & What Is This Dashboard?

The MOH Performance Monitoring Dashboard brings together health data from across the country into one place, so you can track how services are performing — from the national level down to an individual facility.

ℹ️ Who this is for
Health experts, program managers, and decision-makers who need a fast, visual way to check performance against targets — without needing to write queries or understand the underlying database.

At a glance, the dashboard gives you:

What Detail
📁 Sections 8 main modules (Summary, Data Quality, Monthly, Quarterly, Multi Source, Triangulation, Health Intelligence, AI Assistant)
📊 Indicators 94 monthly + 19 quarterly indicators, selected by stakeholders from DHIS2
🔗 Data sources 7 integrated systems (DHIS2, eCHIS, EDHS, MFR, GSM, WUENIC, IGME)
🎚️ Filters 22 filter options across time, geography, and program area

It runs on Apache Superset, an open-source analytics tool — you don't need to know this to use the dashboard, but if you see the name "Superset" in a browser tab or URL, that's why.

🗺️ Finding Your Way Around

Logging In

Step 1. Open Chrome or Firefox and go to your organization's dashboard URL.

Step 2. Enter the Username and Password you received by email.

Step 3. Click Sign In.

The Landing Page

Once you're logged in, you'll land on the main portal page. Every module is one click away from here.

Click any card's View [Module] button (or the arrow icon) to open it. Once inside a module, a tab bar near the top lets you jump between modules without going back to the landing page.

🎛️ Filters, Explained

Most modules share the same basic filtering pattern, usually along the left side of the page.

Filter Narrows the data by...
Period Year, Quarter, or Month
Dataset Which reporting dataset the indicators come from
Program group / Sub-program Thematic area, e.g. Maternal Child and Adolescent Health (MCAH), HIV, Nutrition
Org Unit Geography — Region, Zone, or Woreda

How to filter:

  1. Open the filter panel (usually on the left).
  2. Choose your Period, Dataset, Program area, and/or Org Unit.
  3. Click Apply filters.
  4. Charts and cards update automatically to reflect your selection.

⚠️ Note
Some filters depend on each other — for example, the Org Unit list may only show Zones after you've picked a Region. If a dropdown looks empty, check that a broader filter above it is already selected.

📈 Reading Cards, Charts & Colors

The dashboard reuses a consistent visual language across every module:

Element What it shows How to read it
KPI / value cards A single number for Baseline, Performance, or Target Compare Performance directly against Target to see if you're on track
Bar charts Performance across time periods or regions Taller bar = higher value; compare bar heights side by side
Line charts Trend of an indicator over time Hover over any point to see the exact value for that month, quarter, or region
Heatmaps Performance intensity across many rows at once (e.g., regions × months) Darker/more saturated color = stronger performance (or the opposite, depending on the indicator) — check the legend

Color convention used throughout:

  • 🔵 Light blue / teal — Baseline
  • 🟦 Dark navy — Performance (current/actual)
  • ➖ Green line — Target

💡 Tip
Hover your mouse over any bar, line point, or heatmap cell — nearly every chart in the dashboard shows an exact value on hover, so you rarely need to guess from the visual alone.

1️⃣ Summary

What it's for: a fast, top-level overview of key indicators for decision-makers — 25 KPIs across 9 thematic areas.

Step 1. From the landing page, locate the Summary card.

Step 2. Click View Summary.

What you'll see: bar and line charts showing performance trend against baseline and target for indicators such as ANC 4, Penta 3, and Full Immunization Coverage. Use the thematic tabs (EPI, Child Health, Maternal Health, Family Planning, DPC, Nutrition, Medical Service, HIV) near the top to switch between areas.

2️⃣ Data Quality

What it's for: checking whether the data being reported is complete and submitted on time — before you trust the numbers in other modules.

Step 1. From the landing page, locate the Data Quality card.

Step 2. Click View Data Quality.

This module has three sub-tabs — Monthly, Quarterly, and Meskot app — and focuses on two measures:

Measure Meaning
Completeness Share of expected reports that were actually submitted
Timeliness Share of reports submitted within the required deadline

Filtering: use the left navigation to filter by Period (Month/Quarter/Year), Dataset, and Org Unit (Region/Zone/Woreda). Charts update automatically once you apply your selection.

3️⃣ Monthly & Quarterly

What it's for: viewing indicators that are reported every month (e.g., Outpatient visits, ANC 4, Contraceptive acceptors, Malaria cases) or every quarter.

Step 1. From the landing page, click View Monthly (or View Quarterly).

Filtering options on the left include:

  • Program group — e.g., Maternal Child and Adolescent Health (MCAH), HIV, Nutrition
  • Sub-program — e.g., MCAH – Maternal Health, Immunization Services, Reproductive Family Planning
  • Period — Month, Quarter, or Year
  • Org Unit — Region, Zone, or Woreda

Analyzing Trends

Both the Monthly and Quarterly dashboards display:

  • Cards for Baseline, Performance, and Target
  • Line charts for performance over time
  • Bar graphs for performance across regions
  • A heatmap showing performance via color saturation

💡 Tip
Mouse over any point on a line chart to see the exact value for that month, quarter, or region.

4️⃣ Multi-Source

What it's for: expanding analysis beyond DHIS2 by bringing in other data sources — surveys (EDHS), international estimates (WUENIC, IGME), and systems (eCHIS, MFR, GSM) — for more complete performance monitoring and validation.

Step 1. From the landing page, locate the Multi Source card.

Step 2. Click View Multi Source.

The tabs at the top take you to a page for each data source: WUENIC, IGME, EDHS, MFR, and Other Dashboards. Select the indicator you want (e.g., BCG, Penta 3, Measles) — the chart shows how it has trended over time across multiple sources, so you can assess long-term patterns and data reliability.

5️⃣ Triangulation

What it's for: comparing performance across multiple data sources — DHIS2 vs. EDHS, and DHIS2 vs. eCHIS — to validate data consistency, spot divergences, and detect patterns that may signal reporting issues or genuine changes in service delivery.

Step 1. Review the chart — each bar or line represents the same indicator, but from a different data source.

Step 2. Apply additional filters (e.g., Year) and click Apply Filters to update the view.

6️⃣ Health Intelligence

What it's for: a "dashboard of dashboards" — cross-sector data (logistics, HR, PHEM, financing, and more) brought into one unified view for high-level strategic review.

Step 1. From the landing page, locate Health Intelligence.

Step 2. Click View Health Intelligence.

Navigate across program areas: Health Equity, Health Workforce, Health Financing, Supply and Logistics, Blood Donation, PHEM, Infrastructure, PHC, and Digital Systems.

Apply the Period and Org Unit filters for a more detailed, interactive analysis.

7️⃣ AI Assistant

What it's for: exploring health data using plain-language questions instead of manually navigating dashboards — the AI Assistant returns charts, tables, summaries, and insights instantly.

Step 1. From the landing page, locate AI Assistant.

Step 2. Click Open Assistant.

Top Navigation

Tab What it does
Chat Ask questions about health indicators and get charts, tables, and summaries
Anomaly Detect unusual trends or unexpected changes in indicators
Report Generator Automatically generate narrative reports from selected indicators and periods
Help Guidance on using the AI Assistant

Asking Questions

Type your question in plain language at the bottom of the page. The Assistant accepts text or voice, understands natural language in English and Amharic, and automatically identifies the indicator, period, and geographic level you mean.

Example questions:

  • Show full immunization coverage by region.
  • Compare Penta 3 coverage across regions.
  • Which region has the highest ANC first visit coverage?
  • Show maternal health indicators at national level.

Chat History (left panel) lets you start a New Chat, reopen a previous analysis, or continue an earlier conversation.

Interactive results may include bar, line, pie, or donut charts, data tables, and narrative explanations — switch chart types as needed. You can also Download a chart or View raw data table to inspect the exact values behind it.

Anomaly Detection

Helps you spot unusual patterns, unexpected changes, or possible data-quality issues in indicators over time.

Step 1. Select an Indicator — use the Indicator panel on the left to search for and choose the indicator to analyze.

Step 2. Choose an Organizational Unit — pick the geographic/administrative level (Federal, Region, Zone, Woreda, or Health Facility) from the Org Unit Hierarchy panel.

Step 3. Run the Analysis — click Run to start anomaly detection for your selection.

Step 4. Review the Results — the Across Time Anomalies tab flags unusual increases, decreases, or deviations from historical trends in the current fiscal period. The Unexpected Datasets tab flags datasets that look inconsistent with expected reporting patterns.

Step 5. Interpret Feedback Alerts — the Feedback Alerts panel summarizes anomalies by priority (High, Medium, New) for quick triage.

Report Generation

The Report Generator creates structured analytical reports automatically — from your own analysis or a provided template. You can select reporting periods and indicators, produce narrative summaries backed by charts and tables, and export the result for sharing.

📚 Where the Data Comes From

The dashboard integrates 7 data sources into one analytics platform:

Source Description
DHIS2 Primary source — the national HMIS platform for routine health data reporting from all facilities
eCHIS Electronic Community Health Information System — community-level data from health extension workers
EDHS Ethiopia Demographic and Health Survey — nationally representative household surveys covering fertility, maternal and child health, nutrition, immunization, HIV, and mortality
MFR Master Facility Registry — standardized facility information, including type, services, human resources, and DHIS2 integration status
GSM Gesaportal Modeling for Malaria — combines DHIS2 malaria surveillance and climate data to predict risk and guide interventions
WUENIC WHO/UNICEF Estimates of National Immunization Coverage — standardized annual estimates from administrative data, surveys, and expert review
IGME UN Inter-agency Group for Child Mortality Estimation — harmonized under-five, infant, and neonatal mortality estimates

⚠️ Not specified in the source manual
The manual does not state how frequently each source refreshes, or an exact "as of" data cut-off date shown on-screen. If this matters for your reporting, confirm it with the dashboard administrator before citing figures externally.

💡 Tips for Getting the Most Out of the Dashboard

  • Start with Data Quality before trusting numbers elsewhere — check Completeness and Timeliness for your area first.
  • Hover, don't guess — nearly every chart shows an exact value on hover.
  • Use Triangulation or Multi-Source when a number looks surprising, to see whether other sources agree.
  • Ask the AI Assistant when you want an answer fast and don't want to hunt through tabs and filters manually.
  • Re-apply filters after switching modules — filter selections don't always carry over automatically between modules.
